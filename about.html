Who We Are

Born From a Vision
of Beautiful Spaces.

US Video Walls has its roots in our long-time sister company Medical Murals, who specialize in making beautiful LED faux windows for medical offices and hospitals. With over 100,000 different pictures — many from renowned photographers — we have learned how to make these things look as real as can be.

That expertise in creating believable, immersive visual environments translates directly into our video wall systems. We don't just install screens — we create windows into any world you can imagine.

Our Story

Founded on a Love of Flowing Water

Since 2004, US Water Walls has been the trusted name in luxury water feature design. What began as a single artisan passion for the calming beauty of moving water has grown into a nationwide studio serving discerning clients in all 50 states.

Every piece we create is a collaboration between your vision and our craft — engineered with precision, finished by hand, and built to last a lifetime.

  • ✓ Over 500 bespoke installations delivered
  • ✓ Master craftsmen with 20+ years experience
  • ✓ Premium marine-grade materials only

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How much does a custom water feature cost?+

Pricing depends on size, materials and complexity. Compact features start around $2,500, signature bubble walls and floor fountains typically range $7,500 to $25,000, and grand bespoke installations are custom-quoted. We provide a free, detailed quote for every project.

How long does design and installation take?+

You will receive a 3D concept within 48 hours of your consultation. Once approved, most builds take 3 to 6 weeks depending on scale, followed by a 1 to 2 day white-glove installation.

Do you serve my area?+

Yes — we design and install water features in all 50 US states. Our team travels nationwide for installation and offers remote design consultations.

How much maintenance is required?+

Very little. Our recirculating systems are designed for easy upkeep — typically a water top-up and occasional cleaning. Every install includes our lifetime maintenance program and support.

Can water features be installed outdoors?+

Absolutely. Many of our floor fountains and stone features are built for both indoor and outdoor use with weather-resistant materials and pumps.

FAQ

Common Questions

What's the minimum and maximum size for a video wall? +

There's no practical minimum or maximum — we've installed systems as small as two panels and as large as full wall arrays spanning 30+ feet. We custom design for your exact space. Tell us your wall dimensions and we'll design the optimal configuration.

Can I connect multiple different input sources? +

Yes — we connect cable TV, satellite, streaming boxes, gaming consoles, PCs, Blu-ray players, and any other HDMI source. We can also configure systems to switch between sources on a schedule or with a single remote button.

How long does a typical installation take? +

Most residential installations are completed in 1–2 days. Larger commercial projects may take 3–5 days depending on the scope. We'll give you a precise timeline before work begins so you can plan accordingly.

Do you serve my area? +

Yes — we serve all 50 states. Our installation teams travel nationwide. Call us at 888-205-9035 or send a message with your location and project details, and we'll confirm availability and scheduling.

What's the difference between US Video Walls and Medical Murals? +

Medical Murals (our sister company) specializes in LED faux windows for hospitals and medical offices — displaying beautiful scenic images that reduce patient stress. US Video Walls handles full video wall systems with live video inputs for homes and commercial spaces. We share the same expertise and image library of 100,000+ photographs.
